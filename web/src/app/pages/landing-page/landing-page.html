Forever free · Open source · Self-hosted

Collect feedback without the paywall.

OpenSurvey is a survey and feedback tool you run yourself. Build a form, share a link, read the results. No plans, no caps, no upgrade prompts. Just the whole product, free, for everyone.

Why forever free?

Most survey tools give you a taste and sell you the rest. OpenSurvey starts from the opposite assumption: a feedback tool is infrastructure, and infrastructure should be shared.
